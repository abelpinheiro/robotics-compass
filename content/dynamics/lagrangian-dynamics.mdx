## Formal definition

### Kinetic and potential energy of a link

{/* TODO(author): the kinetic energy comes from the rigid-body lesson; g is the gravity vector in the base frame. */}

$$
T_i = \tfrac12\,m_i\,v_{C_i}^{T}v_{C_i} + \tfrac12\,{}^{i}\omega_i^{T}\,{}^{C_i}I_i\,{}^{i}\omega_i,
\qquad
U_i = -\,m_i\,\mathbf{g}^{T}\,{}^{0}P_{C_i}
$$

### Kinetic energy of the manipulator

{/* TODO(author): Jacobians of the center of mass of each link. */}

$$
v_{C_i} = J_{v_i}(q)\,\dot q, \qquad \omega_i = J_{\omega_i}(q)\,\dot q
$$

$$
T = \tfrac12\,\dot q^{T} M(q)\,\dot q,
\qquad
M(q) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( m_i\,J_{v_i}^{T}J_{v_i} + J_{\omega_i}^{T}\;{}^{0}_{i}R\;{}^{C_i}I_i\;{}^{0}_{i}R^{T}\,J_{\omega_i} \right)
$$

### The Lagrangian and the Euler–Lagrange equations

{/* TODO(author): L is a difference, not a balance; τ gathers the torques that do not come from U. */}

$$
\mathcal{L}(q,\dot q) = T(q,\dot q) - U(q),
\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot q_k} - \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial q_k} = \tau_k
$$

### General form for the manipulator

<Proof title="From Euler–Lagrange to the matrix form">
<ProofStep>

$$
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot q_k} = \sum_j M_{kj}\,\dot q_j
$$

<Why>{/* TODO(author): U does not depend on q̇; M is symmetric. */}</Why>
</ProofStep>
<ProofStep>

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot q_k} = \sum_j M_{kj}\,\ddot q_j + \sum_{i,j} \frac{\partial M_{kj}}{\partial q_i}\,\dot q_i\,\dot q_j
$$

<Why>{/* TODO(author): M depends on q, which depends on time. */}</Why>
</ProofStep>
<ProofStep>

$$
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial q_k} = \tfrac12 \sum_{i,j} \frac{\partial M_{ij}}{\partial q_k}\,\dot q_i\,\dot q_j - \frac{\partial U}{\partial q_k}
$$

<Why>{/* TODO(author) */}</Why>
</ProofStep>
<ProofStep>

$$
\sum_j M_{kj}\,\ddot q_j + \sum_{i,j} c_{ijk}\,\dot q_i\,\dot q_j + \frac{\partial U}{\partial q_k} = \tau_k,
\qquad
c_{ijk} = \tfrac12\left( \frac{\partial M_{kj}}{\partial q_i} + \frac{\partial M_{ki}}{\partial q_j} - \frac{\partial M_{ij}}{\partial q_k} \right)
$$

<Why>{/* TODO(author): symmetrize the sum in i and j (Christoffel symbols). */}</Why>
</ProofStep>
</Proof>

$$
C_{kj}(q,\dot q) = \sum_i c_{ijk}\,\dot q_i, \qquad g_k(q) = \frac{\partial U}{\partial q_k}
$$

### Total energy and work of the motors

{/* TODO(author): this one is an energy balance; it serves to check the result. */}

$$
E = T + U, \qquad \dot E = \dot q^{T}\tau
$$

## Worked example

### One link

{/* TODO(author): θ measured from the horizontal. */}

$$
\mathcal{L} = \tfrac12\,(m\,l_c^2 + I)\,\dot\theta^2 - m\,g\,l_c\sin\theta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\tau = (m\,l_c^2 + I)\,\ddot\theta + m\,g\,l_c\cos\theta
$$

### The 2R arm

{/* TODO(author): energies, derivatives and torques. */}

$$
\begin{aligned}
T ={}& \tfrac12 (m_1 l_{c1}^2 + I_1)\,\dot\theta_1^2 + \tfrac12 I_2 (\dot\theta_1+\dot\theta_2)^2 \\
&+ \tfrac12 m_2 \left[ l_1^2\dot\theta_1^2 + l_{c2}^2(\dot\theta_1+\dot\theta_2)^2 + 2\,l_1 l_{c2}\cos\theta_2\,\dot\theta_1(\dot\theta_1+\dot\theta_2) \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
U = m_1\,g\,l_{c1}\sin\theta_1 + m_2\,g\left( l_1\sin\theta_1 + l_{c2}\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2) \right)
$$
