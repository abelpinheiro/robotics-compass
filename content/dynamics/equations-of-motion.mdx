## Formal definition

### The equation in joint space

{/* TODO(author): the physical meaning of each term. */}

$$
\underbrace{M(q)\,\ddot q}_{\text{inertia}} + \underbrace{C(q,\dot q)\,\dot q}_{\text{Coriolis and centrifugal}} + \underbrace{g(q)}_{\text{gravity}} = \tau
$$

{/* TODO(author): the two previous lessons reach this same equation. */}

### Properties of M and C

{/* TODO(author): what each property guarantees. The second depends on the choice of C (Christoffel). */}

$$
M(q) = M(q)^{T}, \qquad \dot q^{T} M(q)\,\dot q > 0 \quad \text{for } \dot q \neq 0
$$

$$
\left( \dot M - 2C \right)^{T} = -\left( \dot M - 2C \right)
$$

### Inverse and forward dynamics

{/* TODO(author): inverse dynamics for control; forward dynamics for simulation. */}

$$
\tau = M(q)\,\ddot q + C(q,\dot q)\,\dot q + g(q)
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\ddot q = M(q)^{-1}\left( \tau - C(q,\dot q)\,\dot q - g(q) \right)
$$

{/* TODO(author): M from Newton–Euler, column by column (e_j is the j-th column of the identity). */}

$$
M(q)\,e_j = \mathrm{RNE}\left( q,\ \dot q = 0,\ \ddot q = e_j,\ \mathbf{g} = 0 \right)
$$

### Simulation

{/* TODO(author): numerical integration; explicit Euler as the simplest case. */}

$$
\dot q_{k+1} = \dot q_k + \ddot q_k\,\Delta t, \qquad q_{k+1} = q_k + \dot q_k\,\Delta t
$$

### The equation in Cartesian space

{/* TODO(author): from joint to Cartesian space; needs J square and away from singularities. */}

$$
\mathcal{F} = M_x(q)\,\ddot x + V_x(q,\dot q) + G_x(q)
$$

$$
M_x = J^{-T} M J^{-1}, \qquad
V_x = J^{-T}\left( C\,\dot q - M J^{-1}\dot J\,\dot q \right), \qquad
G_x = J^{-T} g
$$

### Contact at the tip and joint friction

{/* TODO(author): F is the force the robot applies on the environment; viscous (v) and Coulomb (c) friction. */}

$$
\tau = M(q)\,\ddot q + C(q,\dot q)\,\dot q + g(q) + J^{T}F + \tau_{\text{friction}},
\qquad
\tau_{\text{friction},\,i} = v_i\,\dot q_i + c_i\,\operatorname{sgn}(\dot q_i)
$$

## Worked example

### Closed form of the 2R arm

$$
M(q) = \begin{bmatrix}
m_1 l_{c1}^2 + I_1 + m_2\!\left(l_1^2 + l_{c2}^2 + 2 l_1 l_{c2}\cos\theta_2\right) + I_2 & m_2\!\left(l_{c2}^2 + l_1 l_{c2}\cos\theta_2\right) + I_2 \\
m_2\!\left(l_{c2}^2 + l_1 l_{c2}\cos\theta_2\right) + I_2 & m_2 l_{c2}^2 + I_2
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
h = m_2 l_1 l_{c2}\sin\theta_2, \qquad
C(q,\dot q) = \begin{bmatrix} -h\,\dot\theta_2 & -h\,(\dot\theta_1+\dot\theta_2) \\ h\,\dot\theta_1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
g(q) = \begin{bmatrix} (m_1 l_{c1} + m_2 l_1)\,g\cos\theta_1 + m_2 l_{c2}\,g\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2) \\ m_2 l_{c2}\,g\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2) \end{bmatrix}
$$
