## Intuition

In the previous chapter, we learned how to describe the relationship between one robot joint and the next using Denavit-Hartenberg (DH) parameters. But knowing that the "elbow is 30 cm away from the shoulder" isn't enough to perform a real task. We usually need to know where the robot's gripper (the End-Effector) is relative to the ground (the Base) so it can pick up an object.

Forward Kinematics (FK) is the mathematical process of answering one simple question: **"If I set my robot's motors to these specific angles, where will the gripper end up in 3D space?"**

If you know how to get from the Base to Joint 1, and from Joint 1 to Joint 2, and from Joint 2 to the Gripper... you can just chain all those instructions together to find a direct path from the Base straight to the Gripper. In robotics, we chain these steps using matrix multiplication.
